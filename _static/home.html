About Me

Hi, I'm Philip Laskowicz — a Master's student in Computer Science, IoT/IT project engineer, and technologist focused on secure and scalable infrastructure for connected energy systems.

I am enrolled in the Master's program in Computer Science at Universität Kassel, where my focus is Energieinformatik. My current work combines AI/ML, power grids, Cyber-Physical Energy Systems, LoRaWAN, IT/networking, and security for real-world infrastructure.

Alongside my studies, I work as an IoT/IT Project Engineer at Städtische Werke Netz + Service GmbH. I work on IoT solutions, LoRaWAN infrastructure, and Smart City projects where reliable data, secure networks, and practical project execution matter.

I completed my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science in April 2026; the thesis grade and final grade are still pending. Before that, I completed an apprenticeship as an Industrial Electronics Technician as part of a dual study program, giving me a strong practical foundation in electrical engineering and operations.
