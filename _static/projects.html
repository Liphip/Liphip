Expense Tracker

I recently developed an Expense Tracker web app that helps users manage the expenses for social events.

Expense Tracker is a comprehensive web application designed to help you manage and track your expenses efficiently. It is ideal for group expenses, such as trips, events, and shared activities.

The app is open source and available on GitHub . It is built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a focus on user-friendly design and functionality. The app is responsive and works well on both desktop and mobile devices, making it convenient for users to track expenses on the go.

Key features of the Expense Tracker include:

  • Easy Expense Management with tags for categorizing expenses
  • Automatic Calculation of Total Expenses and Individual Shares as well as optimization of payments to minimize the number of transactions
  • User-Friendly Interface with a clean and intuitive design for easy navigation

The Expense Tracker offers easy expense tracking for groups, trips, events, and shared activities. As an open-source project available on GitHub, it welcomes community contributions and improvements.

IoT Dashboard - Städtische Werke Netz + Service GmbH

In my role as Project Engineer IoT/IT, I developed the IoT dashboard for Städtische Werke Netz + Service GmbH . This crucial component showcases the capabilities of the Netz + Service LoRaWAN network as well as other IoT components in the Kassel region. The dashboard, built on the ThingsBoard platform, enables users to view data from various sensors and connected systems in real time. It serves as a public facing interface for citizens and stakeholders to view the data collected by the IoT network and its applications in the city of Kassel.

The IoT Dashboard of Städtische Werke Netz + Service GmbH offers real-time monitoring and insights into various data categories:

  • Environmental Data: Weather stations in Kassel, operated by the Umwelt und Garten Amt and Netz + Service, collect data on air temperature, wind speed, and global radiation. This data helps monitor environmental conditions in Kassel and take appropriate measures.
  • Energy Data: Monitoring of electricity generation and consumption from various energy sources such as photovoltaics, wind power, and hydropower. The data comes from various energy generation facilities, including wind farms, PV systems, and power plants. This information enables efficient management of energy supply and the promotion of sustainable energy sources.
  • Leisure: Information on swimming pools in Kassel, including air quality and the occupancy of disabled parking spaces at Auebad. Additionally, the status of the city laser (Laserscape) is displayed. This data contributes to improving leisure opportunities and the quality of life for citizens.
  • Water Level Measurements: Water levels of the Fulda and various tributaries are recorded by ultrasonic sensors. This data is crucial for flood management and the protection of water resources.
  • Waste Disposal: Stadtreiniger Kassel collects data on the fill levels of various waste containers. This information helps plan waste disposal efficiently and ensure cleanliness in the city.
  • Traffic: Monitoring of traffic volume and parking space occupancy through radar sensors. This data supports traffic planning, helps reduce congestion, and improves traffic safety.

The dashboard offers a user-friendly interface that allows easy visualization and analysis of the data. It is a valuable tool for monitoring and managing urban infrastructure and contributes to improving the quality of life in Kassel.

AUREA Project - Stadt Kassel and Kasseler Verkehrs- und Versorgungs-GmbH

The AUREA project is a collaboration between Stadt Kassel and Kasseler Verkehrs- und Versorgungs-GmbH (KVV) to develop a smart city solution for the city of Kassel. The project is part of the Smart City Kassel and the Model Project Smart Cities initiative, which aims to create innovative and sustainable urban environments through the use of digital technologies.

The goal of the Smart AUREA project is to test and implement new technologies and services that improve the quality of life for citizens and enhance the efficiency of urban infrastructure. The project offers an area for testing of new sensors and devices, as well as a public place for citizen engagement and participation.

In my role as Project Engineer IoT/IT, I am part of the sensor and network team, responsible for the installation, configuration and maintenance of various sensors and devices in the Smart AUREA area. This includes the deployment of LoRaWAN sensors for environmental monitoring, traffic management, and flood prevention.
