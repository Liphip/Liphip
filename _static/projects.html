Expense Tracker

I recently developed an Expense Tracker web app that helps users manage the expenses for social events.

Expense Tracker is a comprehensive web application designed to help you manage and track your expenses efficiently. It is ideal for group expenses, such as trips, events, and shared activities. With features like adding expenses, guests, and tags, and providing detailed overviews and downloadable reports, it simplifies expense management.

The app is open source and available on GitHub. It is built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a focus on user-friendly design and functionality. The app is responsive and works well on both desktop and mobile devices, making it convenient for users to track expenses on the go.

Key features of the Expense Tracker include:

  • Easily add expenses by entering the name and amount, then clicking the add button. The expense will be added to the table, and the total and average will be updated.
  • Delete an expense by clicking the delete button, and the total and average will be updated accordingly.
  • Clear all expenses with the clear button, resetting the total and average for a fresh start.
  • Download a CSV file of your expenses with the download button. The CSV file can be opened in spreadsheet programs such as Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
  • Save your data to a file or load data from a file to ensure your expense records are always up-to-date and accessible.
  • Add guests and tags to categorize your expenses and gain detailed insights into your spending patterns.
  • Overview section provides a comprehensive view of the total expenses, average expenses per guest, and average expenses per tag.
  • Manage your guests and tags efficiently with options to add, clear, and associate them with expenses.

The Expense Tracker offers easy expense tracking for groups, trips, events, and shared activities. It features a clear and intuitive user interface for seamless expense management. As an open-source project available on GitHub, it welcomes community contributions and improvements.

IoT Dashboard - Städtische Werke Netz + Service GmbH

In my role as Project Engineer IoT/IT, I contributed to the development of the IoT dashboard for Städtische Werke Netz + Service GmbH . This crucial component showcases the capabilities of their LoRaWAN network. The dashboard, built on the ThingsBoard platform, enables users to visualize data from sensors and devices distributed across the city of Kassel. These sensors, connected through the LoRaWAN network, are managed by the IoT team at Städtische Werke Netz + Service GmbH.

The IoT Dashboard of Städtische Werke Netz + Service GmbH offers real-time monitoring and insights into various data categories:

  • Environmental Data: Weather stations in Kassel, operated by the Environmental and Garden Office and NSG, collect data on air temperature, wind speed, and global radiation. This data helps monitor environmental conditions in Kassel and take appropriate measures.
  • Energy Data: Monitoring of electricity generation and consumption from various energy sources such as photovoltaics, wind power, and hydropower. The data comes from various energy generation facilities, including wind farms, PV systems, and power plants. This information enables efficient management of energy supply and the promotion of sustainable energy sources.
  • Leisure: Information on swimming pools in Kassel, including air quality and the occupancy of disabled parking spaces at Auebad. Additionally, the status of the city laser (Laserscape) is displayed. This data contributes to improving leisure opportunities and the quality of life for citizens.
  • Water Level Measurements: Water levels of the Fulda and various tributaries are recorded by ultrasonic sensors. This data is crucial for flood management and the protection of water resources.
  • Waste Disposal: Stadtreiniger Kassel collects data on the fill levels of various waste containers. This information helps plan waste disposal efficiently and ensure cleanliness in the city.
  • Traffic: Monitoring of traffic volume and parking space occupancy through radar sensors. This data supports traffic planning, helps reduce congestion, and improves traffic safety.
The dashboard offers a user-friendly interface that allows easy visualization and analysis of the data. It is a valuable tool for monitoring and managing urban infrastructure and contributes to improving the quality of life in Kassel.
