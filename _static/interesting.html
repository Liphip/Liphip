Interesting

This page collects topics I keep coming back to: secure IoT and IT infrastructure, energy informatics, AI/ML, and practical project work for environments where reliability matters.

Current Curiosity

  • Secure and scalable IoT architectures for Smart City, industrial, utility, and network-operator contexts.
  • Energy informatics for power grids, grid operation, and Cyber-Physical Energy Systems.
  • AI/ML methods that can support infrastructure projects, from pattern recognition and evaluation to practical demonstrators.

Questions I Like

  • How can IoT systems stay maintainable when they move from a demonstrator into daily operation?
  • Where can AI/ML create real value for grids, utilities, and operational teams without hiding the engineering tradeoffs?
  • How should networking, monitoring, and security be designed when field devices, dashboards, and organizational processes all meet?

Hands-On Interests

  • LoRaWAN systems, sensors, gateways, data flows, and the operational work needed to keep them useful.
  • Dashboards and data visualization that help people understand infrastructure instead of just displaying measurements.
  • Demonstrators that connect IoT, AI/ML, and energy or utility scenarios in a way people can test and discuss.

Beyond The Technical Layer

I am also interested in the project side: coordination between technical and non-technical stakeholders, reliable handover into operation, and decisions that make systems easier to maintain after the first prototype works.
